Talking Heads — Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026. Talking Heads is a Mac app that listens to what your Mac plays and, if you turn it on, to your microphone, and shows the words as speech bubbles over little animated heads.

The short version

Everything you hear and say is turned into text on your Mac. Talking Heads does not record audio, does not keep transcripts, and does not send audio or text anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What Talking Heads listens to

  • What your Mac plays (videos, calls, music), with the macOS System Audio Recording permission.
  • Your microphone, only if you turn it on in the menu (it is off by default), with the macOS Microphone permission.

The sound is kept in memory for the few seconds it takes to recognize a phrase, then discarded. Nothing is written to disk.

What happens on your Mac

  • Speech recognition (NVIDIA Parakeet or OpenAI Whisper models, run on your Mac).
  • Telling voices apart, and whether a voice sounds like a man or a woman, from the sound of the voice. These voice profiles live in memory only and are forgotten after a few minutes of silence or when the app quits.
  • Translation, if you turn it on, with Apple's on-device Translation framework.
  • Reading the emotion of a phrase from its words, if you turn it on.

What goes over the network

On first use the app downloads its speech and voice models (about 700 MB) from Hugging Face, a public model host. These are plain downloads of model files: no audio, text or personal information is sent. Hugging Face, like any website, sees the request coming from your internet address.

If you choose Apple Speech as the recognizer, recognition is done by Apple's speech service. Where your Mac can do this on the device it does; for some languages macOS may send audio to Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. The default recognizer runs entirely on your Mac. If you turn on translation, macOS may download language files from Apple.

What Talking Heads stores

On your Mac only, in the app's own container:

  • Your settings: which sources to listen to, languages, recognizer, avatar size, display options.
  • The downloaded models.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Children

Talking Heads collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: support@cutthecheese.games.
