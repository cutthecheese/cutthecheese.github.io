SixSeven Tap Game — Support

Tap once to start the timer. Tap again when you believe exactly 6.7 seconds have passed. Hit the target to grow your streak — one miss resets the run.

How to play

  • Tap anywhere on the game screen to start the timer.
  • Tap a second time when the timer reads 6.7 seconds.
  • A hit increases your streak and starts a short cooldown. Tapping during the cooldown begins the next attempt immediately.
  • Swipe left for your local statistics, swipe right for the global leaderboard.

Common questions

Where are my statistics stored?
Starts, hits, failures and your best streak are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes them.
Why is the leaderboard empty?
The global leaderboard is Apple Game Center, so it needs an internet connection and a Game Center sign-in. Without either, the game stays fully playable — you just won't see the board.
Where does my name come from?
Your Game Center display name is used when Game Center is available. Signing in is not required — a guest name is used otherwise.
Are there ads or purchases?
No. This release contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.
How do I remove my leaderboard entry?
Scores live in Game Center, so they are managed through your Apple Account. Open Settings, go to Game Center, and hide or reset your profile there.
