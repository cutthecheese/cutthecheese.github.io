SixSeven Tap Game — Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026. This policy explains how SixSeven Tap Game handles information.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your starts, successful taps, failures, current streak, best streak, a randomly generated device identifier, and your player-name preference locally on your device.

Global leaderboard

The global leaderboard is Apple Game Center. When your best streak is greater than one, the game submits that number to Game Center. We do not run a leaderboard server and we do not receive a copy of your score. Apple handles Game Center scores, ranking and display names under Apple's privacy policy.

Game Center

The game reads your Game Center display name to greet you and to show you on the leaderboard. Signing in is not required — you can play the whole game without Game Center, with a locally generated guest name.

Advertising and tracking

This release does not display advertising, does not include an active advertising provider, and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete the app or reset its data. Your leaderboard score lives in Game Center, so it is managed through your Apple Account — you can hide or reset your Game Center profile in the Game Center settings on your device. We cannot delete it for you because we never hold a copy.

Children

The game is not designed to collect personal information from children. Players should not enter sensitive or identifying information as a display name.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game changes. Questions can be sent to support@cutthecheese.games or submitted through the SixSeven support tracker.
