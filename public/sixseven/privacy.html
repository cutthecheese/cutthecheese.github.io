SixSeven Tap Game — Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026. This policy explains how SixSeven Tap Game handles information.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your starts, successful taps, failures, current streak, best streak, a randomly generated device identifier, and your player-name preference locally on your device.

Global leaderboard

When your streak is greater than one, the game may send the following to our Supabase-hosted leaderboard: a randomly generated device identifier, your Game Center display name or guest name, the name source, the platform, the streak, and the submission time. This information is used only to provide and protect the global leaderboard.

Game Center

If you use Apple Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center account information under Apple's privacy policy. The game reads your Game Center display name to identify you on the leaderboard. Signing in is not required to play.

Advertising and tracking

This release does not display advertising, does not include an active advertising provider, and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete the app or reset its data. Leaderboard entries may be retained to operate the leaderboard. To request deletion of leaderboard data, email support@cutthecheese.games or open a request in the support tracker, and include the player name shown in the game.

Children

The game is not designed to collect personal information from children. Players should not enter sensitive or identifying information as a display name.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game changes. Questions can be sent to support@cutthecheese.games or submitted through the SixSeven support tracker.
