Vesper — Support

Vesper is a small bat who lives on your wrist, keeps the opposite hours to you, and has opinions about it. She is awake all night, and just before she goes to sleep at dawn, she wakes you up.

Setting the alarm

  • Swipe to the Alarm screen and turn on Wake me.
  • Set a time, and optionally the days it should repeat. No days selected means every day.
  • When it goes off she flaps and shrieks, and the haptics get sharper the longer you ignore her.
  • Snooze buys you a few minutes and she counts it. Up ends it and extends your streak.

Common questions

Why does the alarm screen say "open me again before then"?
watchOS only lets an app schedule an alarm up to 36 hours ahead, and only while the app is open. If your next alarm is further away than that — a Mondays-only alarm set on a Tuesday, say — Vesper cannot arm it yet. Open the app any time within 36 hours of the alarm and she arms it properly. A notification is scheduled as a backup either way.
Why is it not as loud as the built-in alarm?
Apple does not give third-party apps the system alarm. Vesper uses the strongest mechanism that is available to it, the same one sleep tracking apps use, but the built-in Alarms app has privileges no other app can request.
She has nothing to say about my activity.
She needs Health permission. Go to Settings inside the app and tap Grant permissions. Without it she still works, but she is limited to remarks about the hour and your snoozing habits.
Can I make her nicer, or worse?
Yes. Settings → Tone has three levels. Gentle is fond and worried about you. Melancholy is the default. Bleak does not soften anything.
Does she need my iPhone?
No. Vesper is a standalone watchOS app and runs entirely on the watch.
Where does my health data go?
Nowhere. The app has no networking code at all. See the privacy policy.
