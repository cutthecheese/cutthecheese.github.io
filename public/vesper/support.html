Vesper — Support

Vesper is a small bat who lives on your wrist, keeps the opposite hours to you, and has opinions about it. She is awake all night, and just before she goes to sleep at dawn, she wakes you up.

Setting the alarm

  • On your Apple Watch, swipe to the Alarm screen and turn on Wake me.
  • Set a time, and optionally the days it should repeat. No days selected means every day.
  • When it goes off she flaps and shrieks, and the haptics get sharper the longer you ignore her.
  • Snooze buys you a few minutes and she counts it. Up ends it and extends your streak.

Keeping her on screen

  • On the watch, open Settings → General → Return to Clock, choose Vesper and turn on Return to App. She then stays on screen for up to an hour after you lower your wrist.
  • With your wrist down she falls asleep on the Always-On screen and turns over once a minute.
  • Add her to your watch face or the Smart Stack (turn the Digital Crown on the face): she is up to something new every twenty minutes, and shows when she will wake you.

The iPhone widget

On iPhone she lives only in a Home Screen widget. The iPhone app is just the widget's settings.

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget, and find Vesper.
  3. Pick a size — small, medium or large. Tap her and she answers with a new line.

In the Vesper app on your iPhone you can choose how bleak her lines may get, how often the picture changes (10 to 60 minutes), and whether she shows her lines at all.

Common questions

Why does the alarm screen say "open me again before then"?
watchOS only lets an app schedule an alarm up to 36 hours ahead, and only while the app is open. If your next alarm is further away than that — a Mondays-only alarm set on a Tuesday, say — Vesper cannot arm it yet. Open the app any time within 36 hours of the alarm and she arms it properly. A notification is scheduled as a backup either way.
Why is it not as loud as the built-in alarm?
Apple does not give third-party apps the system alarm. Vesper uses the strongest mechanism that is available to it, the same one sleep tracking apps use, but the built-in Alarms app has privileges no other app can request.
She has nothing to say about my activity.
She needs Health permission on the watch. Go to Settings inside the watch app and tap Grant permissions. Without it she still works, but she is limited to remarks about the hour and your snoozing habits. The iPhone widget never reads Health; its lines are about the time of day.
Can I make her nicer, or worse?
Yes. On the watch, Settings → Tone; for the widget, the Vesper app on your iPhone. Gentle is fond and worried about you. Melancholy is the default. Bleak does not soften anything.
Does she speak Russian?
Yes — English and Russian. She follows your device's language: on the watch, set it in the Watch app on your iPhone (General → Language & Region); on iPhone you can also pick a language just for Vesper in Settings → Vesper → Language.
Does she need my iPhone?
No. The alarm, her remarks and Health all run on the watch. The iPhone app is only there for the Home Screen widget.
Where does my health data go?
Nowhere. Vesper has no networking code at all. See the privacy policy.

По-русски

Vesper — маленькая летучая мышь на Apple Watch. Она живёт в обратном тебе режиме: всю ночь не спит, а перед тем как уснуть на рассвете, будит тебя.

Как поставить будильник
На часах смахни к экрану «Будильник», включи «Будить меня», выбери время и дни. Если дни не выбраны — каждый день. «Отложить» даёт несколько минут, и она их считает. «Встаю» выключает будильник.
Почему написано «открой меня ещё раз до этого»
watchOS разрешает ставить будильник не дальше чем на 36 часов вперёд и только пока приложение открыто. Открой Vesper в пределах 36 часов до будильника — и она заведётся. Уведомление придёт в любом случае.
Как держать её на экране
На часах: «Настройки» → «Основные» → «Возврат к циферблату» → Vesper → «Вернуться к приложению». Когда опускаешь руку, она засыпает на экране «Всегда включён». Её можно добавить на циферблат и в «Смарт-стопку».
Виджет на iPhone
Нажми и подержи пустое место на экране «Домой», нажми «Править» → «Добавить виджет» и найди Vesper. Нажми на неё — она ответит. В приложении Vesper на iPhone можно выбрать тон, как часто меняется картинка и показывать ли её реплики.
Язык
Vesper говорит по-русски и по-английски и следует языку устройства. Для часов язык меняется в приложении Watch на iPhone, а на iPhone его можно выбрать отдельно для Vesper: «Настройки» → Vesper → «Язык».
Данные
В Vesper нет сетевого кода: данные о здоровье никуда не уходят. Подробнее — в политике конфиденциальности. Вопросы: support@cutthecheese.games.
