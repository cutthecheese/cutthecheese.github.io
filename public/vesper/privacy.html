Vesper — Privacy Policy

Effective August 31, 2026. Vesper is a watchOS app that shows an animated bat, wakes you with an alarm, and comments on your activity.

The short version

Vesper contains no networking code. It cannot send your data anywhere, because it has no way to send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

What Vesper reads

With your permission, Vesper reads the following from Apple Health on your watch:

  • Step count
  • Active energy burned
  • Exercise minutes
  • Stand hours
  • Heart rate and resting heart rate
  • Sleep analysis from last night

It uses these to choose which of its pre-written lines to show you. A step count above ten thousand, for example, makes certain remarks eligible. The numbers are turned into a sentence on your wrist and are used for nothing else.

What Vesper stores

On your watch only, inside the app's own container:

  • Your alarm settings
  • How many times you have hit snooze today, and your streak of mornings without snoozing
  • The last sixty remarks the app showed you, so that it does not repeat itself
  • Your tone preference

Nothing else. This data never leaves your watch, and deleting the app deletes all of it.

What Vesper does not do

  • It does not transmit any data off your device.
  • It does not share your health data with anyone, for any purpose.
  • It does not use your health data for advertising or marketing.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, location, or contacts.
  • It does not use analytics or crash reporting services.

Permissions

Health
Optional. Decline it and Vesper still works — it simply has fewer things to say, and falls back to remarks based on the time of day and your alarm history.
Notifications
Used only for the alarm and the optional bedtime nudge.

You can revoke either at any time in the Settings app on your watch or iPhone.

Children

Vesper is not directed at children, and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address and the date at the top will change. Questions can be sent to support@cutthecheese.games.
